A 34-key split keyboard based on the Ferris Sweep, with a TrackPoint built into the right half.

The build uses low-profile Choc switches, hot-swap sockets, and Nice!Nano controllers. The repository includes the board files, parts list, and build photos, with a separate ZMK firmware configuration linked from the README.
