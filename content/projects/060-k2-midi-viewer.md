See a MIDI file mapped onto a full computer-keyboard layout, with staff notation and a built-in synth so you can hear what you're seeing.

Mute individual tracks, adjust pitch, or connect MIDI hardware to watch incoming notes on the virtual keys. It's also a companion to Keyboard Keyboard: MIDI thru lets the viewer show the notes while passing them on to another instrument.
