An IBM Model M rebuilt as a MIDI instrument by electroNuck and me, keeping the original enclosure.

Hall sensors measure how far each key moves, and the keys follow a Wicki–Hayden musical layout. The presentation follows the build from the keyboard's mechanics to playing it through Synthphone-E.
