An IBM Model M rebuilt as a 100-key MIDI instrument, with Hall-effect keys and rotary controls inside the original enclosure.

The keys use a Wicki–Hayden musical layout. Hall sensors measure how far each key moves, giving the firmware more to work with than a simple on/off switch. The presentation follows the build by electroNuck and me, from the keyboard's mechanics to playing it through Synthphone-E.
